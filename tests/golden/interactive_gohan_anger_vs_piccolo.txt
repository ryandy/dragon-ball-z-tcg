│   Second is the corresponding index in the Physical Attack Table (PAT)                           │

│   Physical damage is calculated as the difference of PAT indexes                                 │
│   e.g. Goku may have power "7(3)", meaning power stage 7/10 and PAT index 3                      │

│   Two numbers are used to describe an attack's DAMAGE                                            │
│   These numbers correspond to the effect on your power stages and life deck                      │
│   e.g. Damage(1, 4) is 1 stage of power damage and 4 draws of life damage                        │
